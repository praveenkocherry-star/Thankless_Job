            HOME

            Home has two panels. The first is for Kramdex and the second for replyO. Tap + Kramdex to create a deck: enter its name, and the deck opens on its Configure screen. Tap + replyO to create a container: enter its name, and it opens on its Configure screen. Tap a name in a list to open it. In Kramdex the pencil on the left renames and the red bin on the right deletes.

            SETTINGS

            Choose one of the colour themes from the list. The theme applies to the whole of Lumidex, including Kramdex and replyO. Add your free Google API keys: create a key at Google AI Studio, copy it, and paste it here with Add key. Every saved key works at the same time in Kramdex and replyO.

            BACKUP

            Press Set up backup and choose a folder. Lumidex then saves automatically after each change in Kramdex or replyO, and each backup appears in the list with its date and time. Press Restore on a backup to add it beside what you already have; restored items carry the date and time of the backup. The red bin deletes a backup or the location.

            The Backup section works only in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. If you cannot see the Backup section, your browser does not support backups, so please open Lumidex in Chrome or Edge.
