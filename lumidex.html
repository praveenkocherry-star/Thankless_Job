          CREATE A DECK

          On Home, press + New Deck and name it. Open the deck to reach Configure, where the content is added.

          ADD CONTENT

          Add documents, camera photos and videos, web or video links, or pasted text. Every item you add appears as its own entry in the contents list, and every entry is used when the deck is made. Remove an entry with its cross.

          WHAT YOU GET

          Audiobooks read the study material aloud. Flashcards let you go through the material one card at a time. The Quiz tests what you have learned.

          HOW IT HELPS

          Students and researchers can gather material, reflect on it with flashcards and quizzes, and revise until the material is learned.
