        About

        Lumidex

        Lumidex is a study app for anyone who has to learn a subject fast. You collect what you have: documents, photos, videos, audio, links, your own typed notes, or a spoken question. Lumidex reads it all and turns it into flashcards and a quiz.

        Research, Reflect and Revise

        Research the topic by adding your material or asking a question by voice. Reflect on it with the flashcards. Revise it with the quiz, which shows you what you missed.

        Made for you

        Each deck holds one subject. The flashcards and the quiz are made together, at the largest number the material supports, as one mixed set of easy, medium and hard.

        Your data stays with you

        Your decks are saved on this device and open without the internet. The AI work uses your own free Google key. You can also keep a backup copy in a folder you choose.

        Help

        The basics

        Lumidex keeps your study material in decks. Every deck has three sections at the bottom: Configure, Flashcards and Quiz. Configure is where you add material. Flashcards and Quiz are made from it.

        The top bar

        • The sun or moon button switches between light and dark.
        • The question mark button opens this Help page, from any screen.
        • The three lines button opens the Menu: API keys, Backup and About.

        First, add a Google key

        The AI work needs a free Google key. Without it Lumidex still makes simple cards from your typed text, but not from documents, pictures, audio or links.

        1. Open the Menu and choose API keys.
        2. Tap Create free Google API key. Sign in with your Google account. The first time only, tap Import projects, choose the projects listed and tap Import.
        3. Tap Create API key, choose a project and copy the key.
        4. Come back to Lumidex, tap Save key, paste the key and tap Save.

        You can save more than one key. Lumidex tests each key when you save it. The copy button puts a key on your clipboard. The bin button deletes it.

        Create a deck

        1. On the home screen tap the amber plus button at the bottom right.
        2. Type a name, or accept the suggested one, and confirm.
        3. The deck opens on Configure.

        Decks appear as square tiles, three in a row. Tap a tile to open the deck. The pencil on a tile renames the deck. The bin deletes it, after asking you to confirm. A long name is shortened on the tile and shown in full when you open the deck.

        Add material in Configure

        Configure has four buttons in a row: Documents, Camera, Links and Mic. Below them is a text box. Everything you add appears in the list Contents in this deck. Tap the red cross on an item to remove it. Every item in the list is used.

        Documents

        Tap the document button, then Choose documents. Pick PDF, Word, Excel, CSV or text files from your device. You can pick several at once.

        Camera, photos and video

        • Upload from device: pick photos or videos already on your phone.
        • Start camera: the camera fills the screen. Use the round buttons to take a photo or to start and stop a video. Take as many as you want. Tap the tick to finish. All shots from one visit are saved as one item.
        • Cancel leaves the camera without saving.

        Links

        Tap the links button. There are three boxes: web links, YouTube video links and audio links. Paste a full link starting with https:// and tap the add button beside it. Reading links needs a Google key.

        Mic and audio

        • Record voice content: speak your notes. There is no time limit. Silence is trimmed automatically. Tap Stop, name the recording and save it.
        • Ask a question or research a topic: speak your request. Lumidex researches it online and saves the answer as content.
        • Upload audio: add audio files from your device.

        Voice needs a Google key.

        Typed text

        Type or paste text into the box, then tap Add content. It joins the list as one item.

        Flashcards and Quiz

        Tap Flashcards or Quiz. Lumidex makes both together, in one go, as many as your material supports. A note on the screen tells you while it works, and you may leave the deck meanwhile: the work carries on. If nothing is in the deck yet, you are asked to add material first.

        If your material changes (you add, remove or edit something), the cards and the quiz are made again the next time you open Flashcards or Quiz. If something goes wrong, a message tells you why. Tap Flashcards or Quiz again to retry.

        Using the flashcards

        • Tap the card, or swipe left or right, to turn it.
        • Swipe up for the next card and down for the previous one.
        • After turning a card tap Got it or Missed it. A missed card comes back after a few more.
        • The speaker button reads the card aloud. Tap it again to mute.

        Using the quiz

        • Choose an answer, type one, or match the pairs, then check.
        • You see at once whether you were right, with a short explanation.
        • A wrong answer returns after a few more questions. Your score counts the first try only.
        • At the end you see the questions you missed.

        Backup

        Open the Menu, then Backup. This works in Chrome and Edge only.

        1. Tap Set up backup and choose a folder. A folder inside Google Drive, Dropbox or another cloud app on your device works too.
        2. Lumidex saves a copy after changes. Each copy is listed with its date and time.
        3. Tap Restore to bring a copy back. Your current decks stay and the restored ones are added beside them.
        4. The bin button deletes a copy. Removing the location asks whether to delete the backups as well.

        The Back button

        • In Flashcards or Quiz, Back returns to that deck's Configure.
        • In Configure, Back returns to the home screen.
        • Inside a camera, link, mic or document screen, Back returns to Configure.
        • On the home screen, Back leaves the app.

        Light and dark

        Tap the sun or moon button at the top. Lumidex remembers your choice.

        Good to know

        • Everything is saved on this device and decks open without the internet. The AI work needs the internet.
        • Longer material gives more cards and questions.
        • If the AI is busy, wait a minute and tap again.
