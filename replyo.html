    About replyO

    replyO listens to your spoken question and answers it as a PDF, a Picture, a Voice recording, or all three.

    What it does

    • Writes down what you say and corrects the wording with AI.
    • Answers as a PDF, a Picture or a Voice recording. You can tick more than one.
    • Lets you choose the length: MIN is short and to the point, STD is descriptive, MAX is as complete as possible.
    • Adds photos, tables, flowcharts and charts to the PDF and the Picture when they help you understand.
    • Keeps every question with its answers in the Questions & Responses list.
    • Lets you share any answer, including the voice file, with other apps.

    How to use it

    1. Open Settings and add a free API key from Google, OpenAI or Hugging Face.
    2. Go to Home and tick PDF, Picture, Voice, or any mix.
    3. Move the slider to MIN, STD or MAX.
    4. Tap Ask a question, speak, then tap Stop and send.
    5. Wait for the message that says Done. Your answers appear in the list.
    6. Tap Open to view an answer, Share to send it, or Listen to hear the voice. The voice never starts by itself.
    7. Open Backup now and then to keep a copy of everything.

    Good to know

    • You can save several keys. With more than one key, replyO works faster because the keys share the job.
    • Voice files are made with a Google or OpenAI key. A Hugging Face key is used for the written answers.
    • Photos come from Wikimedia Commons, and each photo is credited. If no photo is found, a graphic is used instead.
    • Voice input works when replyO is opened from its web address, not from a saved file.

    Your data

    Everything stays in this browser on this phone. A backup includes your API keys, so keep backup files private. Clearing the browser data removes your answers unless you have a backup.
