How to do this UAT

  1. Open jixcy-foods.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Use a phone for the phone column and a laptop or desktop for the desktop column.
  2. Work through the areas in order. Each test lists the steps and what should happen.
  3. For each test, mark Pass, Fail, Blocked (you could not run it) or Not applicable, separately for phone and desktop.
  4. For every Fail or Blocked, write what happened in the comment box. Screenshots can be emailed separately; mention the test ID, for example S4.
  5. At the end of each area, rate it from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). At the bottom, give your overall verdict.
  6. Your answers save in this browser as you go, so you can stop and continue later in the same browser. To continue on another device, use Download backup and then Load backup on the other device.
  7. When finished, press Download results and email the file to the project owner. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
